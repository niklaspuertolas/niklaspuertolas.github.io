About 🧡

I’m a product designer who loves turning messy problems into tools that feel simple, and sneaking a bit of fun into them. Currently designing at Landbot.

Before product, I spent years in audiovisual production, branding and web. That’s where I learned to think about the person on the other side of the screen.

Lately I’ve gone deep on AI: I’m a certified AI Product Designer through The Hero Camp’s AI Product Designer Pro program.

I learn by building, and I’m happiest when something I made quietly makes someone’s day easier ✌️
