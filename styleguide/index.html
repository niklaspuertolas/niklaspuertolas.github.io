Styleguide

Design system v1 (Phase 2, approved 2026-09-27). Everything here renders from src/styles/tokens.css and the components in src/components/. Resize the window: type and spacing are fluid between 375px and 1440px.

Approved deviations from Figma

All approved by Nik on 2026-09-27.

  1. P1

    Warm near-black instead of pure #000

    Text goes from #000 to #1f1c19 so it sits on the cream instead of cutting through it. Contrast stays at 16.4:1.

  2. P2

    Darker nav and metadata greys (accessibility)

    The nav links in Figma (#979797-ish, 2.9:1) and the metadata greys (3.2–3.5:1) fail WCAG AA. Nav moves to --color-text-muted (5.7:1), metadata to --color-text-subtle (5.0:1).

  3. P3

    Softer sticker shadow

    Figma uses one hard shadow (0 4px 4px, 25% black). The proposal is two warm layers, a tight contact shadow plus a wide soft one, so the objects feel like they’re lying on paper.

  4. P4

    Unified section titles

    “Currently cooking” / “Other Work” are 20px and “About” is 24px in Figma. All section titles share one role: 20px mobile → 24px desktop.

  5. P5

    Bigger hero headline

    Figma’s 56px → 64px on desktop, for more presence against the stickers.

  6. P7

    Pill buttons

    The project-page wireframe had square buttons. Pills match the friendly tone of the site.

Other system decisions

  • DM Sans is the only typeface (no SF Pro in the nav, no monospace).
  • No dark mode. The cream canvas is part of the identity.
  • English only in v1: the Figma language selector is not built.
  • The nav floats and exists only on the home; its links jump to home sections. Case studies get a back-to-home link instead.
  • Project cards: the screenshot itself is the image, then title, a one-to-two-line summary and tags. No frame, no card box, no metric.
  • Icons come from Hugeicons (free set). Images ship as WebP generated from the PNG originals.
